مدة الحفظ

  • اسمك المستعار وتقدمك ونتائجك وموافقتك على البنود: ما دامت المزامنة مفعّلة، وتُحذف كلها فور طلبك.
  • حسابك المرتبط بـ Google (بريدك واسمك): إلى أن تحذف بياناتك من التطبيق، فيُحذف الحساب نفسه مع آخر اسم مستعار له.
  • «المساعد»: الأجوبة المولَّدة العامة تُحفظ للاستعمال المتكرر ما دامت نصوص المكتبة وبنك الأسئلة لم تتغير؛ والنازلة التدريبية المولَّدة ثلاثة أيام؛ والتمارين المولَّدة لك لا تُحفظ على خادمنا؛ ووقائع النوازل والصور والتسجيلات ونصوص التحرير لا تُحفظ أصلاً؛ وسجله التقني وخطتك ونوازلك التدريبية تُحذف مع بياناتك. وبطاقات المراجعة ومحفوظاتك من النصوص القانونية جزء من تقدمك.
  • الإحصاءات: وفق مدة الاحتفاظ المضبوطة في Firebase (شهران).
  • البلاغات عن الأسئلة: إلى أن يُراجع السؤال المعني.

حقوقك وكيف تمارسها

لك الحق في الولوج إلى معطياتك وتصحيحها وحذفها والتعرض على معالجتها، وفق القانون رقم 09.08 المتعلق بحماية الأشخاص الذاتيين تجاه معالجة المعطيات ذات الطابع الشخصي.

  • الحذف من الخادم: في التطبيق ← «المزيد» ← «الحساب والخدمات السحابية» ← «حذف بياناتي من السحابة». يُحذف اسمك المستعار وتقدمك وأجوبة سؤال اليوم ونتائج التحديات وسجل «المساعد» فوراً، ومعها حسابك المرتبط بـ Google (بريدك واسمك) إن لم يبقَ له اسم مستعار آخر. والطريقة دون التطبيق، وما يُحذف وما يبقى، في صفحة حذف البيانات.
  • إيقاف الإحصاءات: «المزيد» ← «الإعدادات» ← «إحصاءات الاستعمال».
  • الإعلانات: «المزيد» ← «الإعلانات» ← «خيارات الخصوصية للإعلانات» (حيث يلزم ذلك قانوناً)؛ ويمكنك إعادة ضبط معرّف الإعلانات أو حذفه من إعدادات أندرويد (الخصوصية ← الإعلانات)، و«ساعة دون إعلانات» متاحة في الصفحة نفسها.
  • نسخة من معطياتك: «المزيد» ← «النسخة الاحتياطية» يصدّر تقدمك كاملاً في ملف.
  • الحذف من الجهاز: إلغاء تثبيت التطبيق أو مسح بياناته.

Privacy policy

The Qanoun Tahiro app (Android, iPhone and web) and its website · Last updated: 23 November 2026

In short: you enter the app in one of two ways of your choice: an alias you pick, or your Google account; we never ask for your name or phone number. If you choose Google, we receive your email and name only to identify your account, and they are never shown to anyone: leaderboards show a name generated by the app (e.g. “Student 4821”). The Assistant (AI, running on Google’s Gemini model) works only when you ask it to, and is for people aged 18 or over; what you send it (a question, a photo of a question, or a recording of your oral answer) is processed and then forgotten, and Google does not use it to improve its models. The Android app shows Google AdMob ads that fund the free service (no ads in the iPhone app, on the website or in the web version), personalised only with your consent where the law requires it. No data is ever sold. Usage analytics run only with your consent, and the iPhone app has no analytics and no tracking.

Ahmed Bakkali Tahiri (Tahiro), independent developer, Tetouan — Morocco. To contact us about your data: through the developer details on the app’s Google Play or App Store page, or from within the app.

What we do not collect

  • Your phone number, contacts, photos and recordings (the question photo and the oral-answer recording are processed and then forgotten, never stored), or precise location; nor your real name or email address unless you choose Google sign-in.
  • We never sell or rent any data, and we never pass your alias, email, progress or answers to AdMob.

Service providers

The data above is processed by: Supabase (database, sessions and authentication), PubNub (live presence), Ably (live broadcast from the server) and Google (Google sign-in if you choose it; Firebase for analytics and website hosting; AdMob for ads in the Android app, under Google’s policy on partner sites; and the Gemini API, which generates the Assistant’s answers and exercises: we use the paid tier, under whose terms Google does not use what is sent to it or its answers to improve its products, keeping them only for a limited time to detect abuse and as required by law, and every call asks that it not be logged in our project). Their servers may be located outside Morocco, which is a transfer of data abroad, limited to the purposes stated.

Retention

  • Your alias, progress, results and terms acceptance: for as long as sync is on; all of it is deleted as soon as you ask.
  • Your Google-linked account (email and name): until you delete your data from the app, when the account itself is deleted together with its last alias.
  • The Assistant: general generated answers are kept for reuse as long as the library texts and question bank are unchanged; a generated practice case, for three days; exercises generated for you are not stored on our server; case facts, photos, recordings and essay texts are never stored; its technical log, your plan and your practice cases are deleted with your data. Review cards and saved law articles are part of your progress.
  • Statistics: according to the retention period set in Firebase (two months).
  • Reports about questions: until the question concerned has been reviewed.

Your rights and how to exercise them

You have the right to access, rectify and delete your data and to object to its processing, under Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data.

  • Deletion from the server: in the app → “More” → “Account & cloud services” → “Delete my cloud data”. Your alias, progress, daily-question answers, challenge results and Assistant log are deleted immediately, together with your Google-linked account (email and name) if it has no other alias. How to do it without the app, and what is deleted or kept, is explained on the data deletion page.
  • Turning off statistics: “More” → “Settings” → “Usage statistics”.
  • Ads: “More” → “Ads” → “Ad privacy options” (where required by law); you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Privacy → Ads), and “One ad-free hour” is available on the same page.
  • A copy of your data: “More” → “Backup” exports your full progress to a file.
  • Deletion from the device: uninstall the app or clear its data.

Security

Server tables are closed to direct reading and writing; the app reaches them only through functions that check that the alias belongs to your device or account. Google sign-in happens directly with Google in the system browser, so the app never sees your password. The recovery code is stored hashed with bcrypt, and recovery attempts are locked after eight wrong attempts per hour. Service secret keys stay on the server and never reach the app.

Children and age

The app is intended for law students and candidates for competitive exams, not for children. The Assistant is reserved for people aged 18 or over (a condition of the Gemini API terms), and you are asked to confirm this before using it for the first time.
